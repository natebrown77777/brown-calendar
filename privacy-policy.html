Privacy Policy

Brown Calendar · Last updated: August 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile application Brown Calendar ("the App") handles your information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

Summary. Everything you create in the App — tasks, notes, reminder times, and settings — is stored only on your own device. The developer operates no server, receives none of your data, and has no way to access anything you store. The App contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking.

Information the App stores on your device

The following is saved locally so it is there the next time you open the App:

  • Tasks you create, including their names, repeat settings, start and stop dates.
  • Reminder times you set for a task, and whether alarms or notifications are switched on.
  • Notes you write for individual days.
  • Your preferences and the current state of the App.

This information is held in the App's local storage on your device. It is not transmitted to the developer and is not accessible to the developer.

Information the App does not collect

The App does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, or any other personal or device information. There is no account to create and no sign-in.

Reminders and notifications

If you switch on a reminder for a task, the App asks Android for permission to show notifications, and may ask for permission to schedule exact alarms so reminders arrive at the time you set rather than several minutes late. You can decline either request, or withdraw it later in your device settings; the rest of the App continues to work.

Reminders are scheduled and delivered entirely by your device. Nothing is sent over the internet, and no reminder passes through any server. A reminder displays the name of the task you wrote and nothing else. Please bear in mind that, like any notification, it may appear on your lock screen and be visible to anyone who can see it — something to consider when naming a task.

Internet access and third parties

The App works fully offline and never sends your tasks, notes, or reminders over the internet.

There is one exception worth stating plainly. The App loads two display typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) when your device has a connection. Like any web request, this reveals your device's IP address to Google, and is covered by Google's own privacy policy. It carries no information about you or anything you have written in the App. If your device is offline, the App substitutes a system typeface and everything else works normally.

The App also contains a link to an external retail website. Opening it leaves the App and takes you to a third-party site governed by that site's own privacy policy.

Device backup

Your device's operating system may include the App's data in Android's standard system backup. Where that happens, the backup is handled by your device and your own Google account: it is controlled by your device settings, governed by Google's privacy policy, and is not operated, accessed, or received by the developer. You can turn it on or off in your device's system settings.

The App itself has no export, import, or cloud-sync feature, and no facility for sending your data anywhere.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age. Because nothing is collected or transmitted, no personal information about a child could be gathered through ordinary use.

Data security

Because your information stays on your device, its security depends chiefly on the security of that device, and protecting it with a passcode or other lock is worth doing. The developer cannot access your data and therefore cannot recover it if your device is lost, reset, or the App is uninstalled without a system backup in place.

Your choices

You are in full control of your data. You can edit or delete any task or note in the App at any time. Uninstalling the App removes its locally stored data from your device. Notification and alarm permissions can be withdrawn at any time through your device settings, and system backups can be managed or disabled there too.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. If it changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Significant changes will be reflected in an updated version of the App where appropriate.

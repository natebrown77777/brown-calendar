How the Brown calendar works
  • The year begins on December 25, the Nativity. That day is the 1st Day of the Year and sits outside the week.
  • Then come 13 months of 28 days: Peter, Andrew, James, John, Philip, Bartholomew, Thomas, Matthew, Jamalpha (James, son of Alphaeus), Thaddeus, Simon, Matthias and Stephen.
  • Every month has exactly four weeks: Lightday, Skyday, Treeday, Starday, Animalday, Humanday, Restday. Day 1 of every month is a Lightday.
  • Leap years follow the Gregorian rule (every 4 years, except centuries not divisible by 400). The extra Leap Day comes at the end of the year, also outside the week.
  • Brown year N runs from December 25 of Gregorian year N − 1 to December 24 of year N.
